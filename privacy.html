Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

J.A.R.V.I.S is a personal assistant used only by its developer, Göktuğ Özışık. It is not available to other users.

What data it uses

With the account owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app can read and manage his own Google Calendar events, Gmail messages and YouTube playlists.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give him reminders, summaries and help with his own schedule, email and videos. It is processed on his own devices and by the AI services the assistant uses to answer him.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than the features above. Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
