J.A.R.V.I.S

J.A.R.V.I.S is a personal assistant app built and used by one person, Göktuğ Özışık, on his own computer and phone. It is not offered to the public.

With his permission, it reads his own Google Calendar, Gmail and YouTube data to remind him about school work, events and messages.
